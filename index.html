Ludus privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Ludus: Sand and Steel is a single-player game by Stonefall Games for Android.

What we collect

We don't collect anything ourselves. Ludus has no accounts and no analytics of its own. Two Google services built into the game handle ads and purchases, as described below.

Ads

Ludus shows an ad only when you tap a button that offers one for a reward. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob collects your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, how you interact with the ads, and crash and performance data. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads. You can change your choice any time in Menu, Game, Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Purchases

Patron of the Ludus is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. Google Play tells the game whether your account owns the purchase, so it can be restored on a new phone.

Your saves

Your progress is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it. We cannot see or recover it.

Permissions

The game asks to vibrate your phone for hit feedback. It uses the internet for ads and for Google Play purchases.

Children

Ludus is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change what the game collects, we will update this page before that version ships and change the date above.
