Ludus privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Ludus: Sand and Steel is a single-player game by Stonefall Games for Android.

What we collect

Nothing. Ludus has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The game does not send your data anywhere.

Your saves

Your progress is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it. We cannot see or recover it.

Permissions

The game asks to vibrate your phone for hit feedback. Android lists internet access because the game engine adds it by default; Ludus does not use the internet.

Children

Ludus is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds ads or purchases, we will update this page before that version ships and change the date above.
